🗺️ How It Works
1 · Register Your Profile
Sign up with your name, department, nationality, and your supported team. Set a 4-digit PIN — this protects your predictions so no one else can change them.
2 · Submit Your Predictions
Go to the Predictions tab and enter your score picks for any match. You can predict the entire group stage in one go — come back anytime to update a pick, as long as it's not locked.
3 · Predictions Lock 1 Hour Before Kick-Off
Once a match reaches its lock window (60 minutes before kick-off), the prediction input closes. No changes accepted after that point — plan ahead!
4 · Results Are Entered by the Admin
After each match, the admin enters the official final score. All points are recalculated instantly and the leaderboard updates in real time.
5 · Knockout Rounds = Higher Stakes
When the tournament enters the knockout phase, points are multiplied! Quarter-finals ×2, Semi-finals ×3, and the Final ×5. One perfect final prediction could change everything.
6 · Check Analytics & Leaderboard
Track your streaks, form, accuracy, and points earned. See how your department is performing. Watch the group table standings update as real results come in.
⚖️ General Rules
⏰
Prediction Deadline
All predictions must be submitted at least 1 hour before kick-off. The system locks automatically — no exceptions.
🔐
PIN Protection
Your 4-digit PIN is personal. Never share it. Only you can update your own predictions. Contact the admin if you forget your PIN.
🏆
Winning
The player with the most points at the end of the tournament wins the Individual Trophy. The department with the highest average wins the Department Shield.
🤝
Tiebreaker
In case of a tie on total points, the player with more exact score predictions wins. Then by number of correct results. Then alphabetically.
📊
Department Score
Department rankings are based on average points per player, so smaller departments compete fairly against larger ones.
🌍
Coverage
This competition covers all 48 group stage matches plus knockout rounds of FIFA World Cup 2026. Knockout matches are added by the admin as teams qualify.
